Distribution Shape Explorer: Skewness, Kurtosis, and the Hidden Geometry of Risk

Distribution Shape Explorer

Mean-variance optimisation focuses on two numbers: the mean and the volatility. But two distributions can share the same mean and volatility and still look completely different, depending on their skewness and kurtosis.

This tool lets you explore that. Four parameters shape the distribution and show how the mean, median and geometric mean can move apart.

The model is illustrative, simplified, and not a forecast.
